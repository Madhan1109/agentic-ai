# Remote Work & Hybrid Policy — Acme Corp HR

**Document ID:** HR-POL-REMOTE-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

## 1. Hybrid Work Model
- Default model: **3 days in office / 2 days remote** per week for eligible roles.
- Core collaboration days: **Tuesday and Thursday** (in-office required unless exception approved).
- Fully remote roles require VP + HR approval and are role-specific.

## 2. Eligibility
- Employees must have completed probation (**90 days**).
- Role must be designated as hybrid-eligible by the department head.
- Performance rating must be “Meets Expectations” or above on the latest review.
- Contractors follow the terms of their Statement of Work.

## 3. Equipment & Expenses
- Company laptop and standard software are provided.
- Home internet stipend: **$50/month** for hybrid-eligible employees.
- Ergonomic equipment requests are reviewed by Facilities/HR (one-time up to **$300** with receipts).

## 4. Expectations
- Be online and responsive during local core hours (**10:00–16:00**).
- Attend mandatory team meetings on camera when remote.
- Protect company data; use VPN for all corporate systems access outside office.

## 5. Temporary Remote Work
- Up to **10 consecutive working days** of temporary remote work may be approved by the manager for travel or personal reasons.
- Longer arrangements require HRBP approval.
