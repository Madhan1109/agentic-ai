# Leave Policy — Acme Corp HR

**Document ID:** HR-POL-LEAVE-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines employee leave entitlements, eligibility, accrual rules, and request procedures at Acme Corp.

## 2. Leave Types

### 2.1 Annual / Privilege Leave (PL)
- Full-time employees: **18 days per calendar year**
- Accrual: **1.5 days per month** of continuous service
- Maximum carry-forward into next year: **5 days**
- Minimum continuous service for first leave request: **90 days**

### 2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
- Full-time employees: **12 days per calendar year**
- Does not carry forward
- Medical certificate required for absences of **3 or more consecutive days**

### 2.3 Casual Leave (CL)
- Full-time employees: **6 days per calendar year**
- Intended for short personal emergencies
- Maximum **2 consecutive days** without manager approval beyond CL balance checks

### 2.4 Maternity Leave
- Eligible after **6 months** of continuous service
- Entitlement: **26 weeks** as per applicable law
- Must notify HR at least **8 weeks** before expected leave start

### 2.5 Paternity Leave
- Eligible after **6 months** of continuous service
- Entitlement: **10 working days**
- Must be taken within **3 months** of childbirth

### 2.6 Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- Granted for approved weekend/holiday work
- Must be availed within **60 days** of accrual
- Requires manager pre-approval of the extra work

## 3. Eligibility Summary
| Employment Type | PL | SL | CL | Maternity | Paternity |
|-----------------|----|----|----|-----------|-----------|
| Full-time       | Yes| Yes| Yes| Yes*      | Yes*      |
| Part-time       | Pro-rated| Pro-rated| Pro-rated| Case-by-case | Case-by-case |
| Contractor      | No | No | No | No | No |
| Probation (<90 days) | Accrues but generally not usable | Limited SL only | No | No | No |

\* Maternity/Paternity require 6 months continuous service.

## 4. Leave Calculation Rules
1. **Half-day leave**: Deducts **0.5** day from the selected leave type.
2. **Working-day basis**: Weekends and company holidays are **not** deducted from leave balance.
3. **Pro-rated entitlement** for mid-year joiners:
   - Formula: `(Annual entitlement / 12) * remaining months in year` (rounded down to nearest 0.5)
4. **Negative balance**: Not permitted except with CHRO/HR Director written approval.
5. **Leave without pay (LWP)**: Available when paid balances are exhausted; impacts payroll and may affect benefits eligibility if > 30 days in a year.

## 5. Request & Approval Workflow
1. Employee submits leave request in HR portal / via HR Chat Agent guidance at least **2 working days** in advance for PL/CL.
2. Sick leave may be applied retrospectively within **2 working days** of return.
3. Approver: Direct manager (up to 5 consecutive days); HRBP for longer durations.
4. Blackout periods (e.g., year-end close, major releases) may restrict PL; exceptions require Director approval.

## 6. Public Holidays
Acme Corp observes **12 public holidays** annually. A separate holiday calendar is published each December for the following year. Public holidays do not consume leave balances.
