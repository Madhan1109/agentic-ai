# Employee Benefits Policy — 12I Corp HR

**Document ID:** HR-POL-BEN-2025  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

## 1. Health Insurance
- Full-time employees and dependents are covered under the corporate group medical plan.
- Coverage begins on the **1st of the month following** joining date (or immediately if joined on the 1st).
- Annual health checkup benefit: up to **$300** reimbursable.

## 2. Retirement / Provident Contributions
- Company matches employee contributions up to **4%** of base salary.
- Vesting: **100% after 12 months** of continuous service.

## 3. Learning & Development
- Annual L&D budget: **$1,000** per full-time employee.
- Certifications relevant to the role are prioritized.
- Requests go through manager + L&D portal.

## 4. Wellness
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): confidential counseling, 24/7.
- Wellness stipend: **$200/year** for fitness or mental wellness apps/gyms.

## 5. Referral Bonus
- Successful referral of a full-time hire: **$1,500** after the new hire completes **90 days**.
